Privacy

The site is designed to work without accounts, advertising trackers, or third-party font requests.

Browser storage

The Mouse FX preference uses session storage and disappears when the browser session ends.

You can clear it at any time by deleting this site’s browser data.

Hosting records

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, requested URL, user agent, timestamp, and security events. Open Engine does not add advertising analytics or cross-site tracking to these pages.

External links

Links to GitHub and Discord take you to services with their own privacy policies. No content from those services is embedded on this site before you choose to visit them.
